Why Check Your Photo Metadata?

Your photos contain more information than you think. Here's what you need to know.

📍

GPS Location

Every smartphone photo embeds your exact GPS coordinates. Anyone who gets the original file can see where you took it — potentially your home address.

📱

Device Fingerprint

Your camera model, phone brand, and even the lens used are recorded. This creates a digital trail of all the devices you own.

🕐

Timestamps & More

Exact date and time, camera settings, editing software used — all embedded invisibly in every image file you share.
